## Learned
- "Year to date" needs a definition: prices, generation and next-morning windows all end on different days; one fixed as-of date per build is the only way zones and marts agree
- A view-based as-of date moved mid-build when raw files changed; making it a table pins it for the whole run
- Double rounding bites: a share stored as 0.7205 printed as 72.1% in one note and 72.0% in another (true value 72.046%). The quoted-number check caught it
- Market rules shape rankings: North Italy's 0 negative hours are a floor in the auction, not the absence of surplus

## Blockers
- None

- Year-to-date vs full year is the most common way a partial year misleads; the fix is structural (a like-for-like mart), not wording
- An automatic number matcher finds a cell for almost any small number by chance; table cells can be pinned to their column, prose needs reviewed entries
- Published counts differ by definition (hourly mean vs period, < 0 vs ≤ 0); matching a source means matching its definition first
