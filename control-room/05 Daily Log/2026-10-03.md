## Learned
- Reporting intervals switch **mid-month**: ES load/generation 2022-05-23, FR Dec 2024, PL load 2024-06-13, all at 00:00 UTC. A monthly majority would mislabel ES's 22 hourly days in May 2022 as 15 min (4× energy error), so resolution is set per day
- TSO data switches at UTC midnight; the day-ahead market switched at CET midnight (22:00 UTC)
- "Sparse" types (pumped storage) only have rows while generating: absence ≠ zero, and a lone point can't tell you its own interval
- PT 2026 "missing" load wasn't missing: PT reports hourly, others 15-min
- ENTSO-E's Polish prices before 2019-11-20 are in **PLN**: the mart's PL 2019 average was 212 "EUR". Plausibility-check averages against neighbours, not just ranges
- Counting rows instead of durations would overstate ES 2026 negative hours 4× (2,737 rows vs 684 h)
- The expected story "Iberia = many, shallow" is only half right for 2025: Iberia is clearly shallower (−2 vs −11 to −14 €/MWh), but NL and DE had slightly *more* negative hours than ES. ES leads only when zero prices count
- 8 categorical colours can't all stay distinct in a scatter (validator: only 4 pass all-pairs), so every chart names zones in text and colour is a consistent second cue
- ENTSO-E's NL "Solar" is ~2% of real Dutch solar (0.49 vs 22 TWh in 2024, CBS): rooftop PV is missing. Always sanity-check a source's totals against national statistics before using shares
- Polish solar only appears in ENTSO-E from 2020-04-10
- Split a capture rate into seasonal × within-month before comparing years: 2022 looked like a reversal but was only timing
- With yearly data, two inputs that both trend up (own vs regional share) can't be separated; would need hourly data
