How to read these numbers

  • Year to date runs from 1 January to the data's as-of date: the last day on which all 8 zones have complete day-ahead prices and generation, the same for every zone. It is compared with the same dates last year.
  • North Italy has a 0 €/MWh floor on its day-ahead market (GME rules), so it cannot show negative prices; rankings that include it partly reflect that rule.
  • Negative hours: time with a day-ahead price below 0 (15-minute periods count as a quarter hour).
  • Solar capture rate: what a MWh of solar earned as a share of the average price. Solar output as reported to ENTSO-E; for the Netherlands that covers about 2% of Dutch solar, so its rate is indicative only.
  • Battery revenue: a 1 MW / 2 MWh battery, at most one cycle a day, 88% round trip, with perfect foresight of cleared day-ahead prices. An upper bound for day-ahead arbitrage only: excludes intraday, balancing and capacity markets, degradation and grid fees. Not investment advice.
  • EV saving: 10 kWh a day at 7 kW, charged in the cheapest block of each day instead of at 18:00. Wholesale price only: no retail margin, taxes or grid fees.
